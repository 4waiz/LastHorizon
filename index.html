      LastHorizon

      A quiet afternoon on a coastal road. There is nowhere you need to be — walk up the hill, look around, and see what people left behind.

      Objective

      Five keepsakes are hidden around the neighbourhood. Walk close to one to pick it up. Your progress is saved in this browser.

      Art and audio

      Design, code, models, animation, shaders and music are first-party, created for this project. Every model in public/assets/models is generated by the Python scripts in scripts/blender — see docs/ASSET_LICENSES.md for the per-file list.

      Audio is both supplied files and synthesis, and it is worth being exact: two first-party music beds ship as MP3 (outdoor.mp3 564 kB, indoor.mp3 1,104 kB) and everything else — the chord bed, footsteps, weather, vehicles, interface — is generated at runtime from oscillators and noise buffers. If either file fails to load the game degrades to synthesis alone.

      No third-party art, audio, model, font or branding is used.

      The game stopped

      Something went wrong and Last Horizon had to stop. Your progress is saved up to the last autosave — reloading should put you back near where you were.

      The diagnostic file stays on your device — nothing is uploaded. It records what broke, your browser and screen size, and where you were in the game. Attach it to a bug report if you would like to.
